About

My academic background and current research direction.

My academic journey in electrical engineering began at the University of Rajshahi, where I completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering, specializing in Electronics. Throughout my studies, I developed a strong interest in photonics, electromagnetic systems, optical devices, and metamaterials.

My research experience includes the design and numerical investigation of photonic devices and electromagnetic structures, with a focus on optical waveguides, microring resonators, photonic integrated circuits, and metamaterials. This work has provided me with a strong foundation in theoretical analysis, computational modeling, and numerical simulation.

In Fall 2026, I joined the PhD program in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Florida International University and became part of the Quantum Technologies and Metamaterials (QTM) Lab under the supervision of Prof. Alex Krasnok. My current research focuses on photonics and quantum technology, with particular interests in light–matter interactions, resonant phenomena, metamaterials, and advanced photonic systems. I am interested in combining analytical theory, computational modeling, simulation, and experimental understanding to investigate problems in modern photonics and electromagnetic engineering.
